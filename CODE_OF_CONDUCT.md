# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in FlowSentry a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and constructive technical feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the reliability and security of the project and its community.
- Refrain from harassment, personal attacks, or publishing private information.

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers may remove or ban contributors who violate this code.
